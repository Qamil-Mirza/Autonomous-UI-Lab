# GhostLoop launch film

**Goal:** A product launch video, under 3 minutes (target about 70 seconds), that leaves a viewer able to say what GhostLoop is and what it does. User delegated creative direction and message; opted out of brainstorming questions ("just start").

**One-sentence takeaway the film is built around:** GhostLoop sends AI users through your real app before launch, shows you where they get stuck, proposes one small change, and measures whether it helped, with you approving every change.

## Why a new film rather than a fifth promo cut

`promo/` holds a 32-second animated promo (v1 through v4) that uses cartoon ghosts and a fictional "Coor" pricing screen. It never shows the real product. A launch film whose job is "know what we do" needs the real dashboard, the real test app, and the real loop on screen. The new film lives in `promo/launch/` and leaves the promo untouched.

## Reference study (what launch videos that do well have in common)

Linear feature launches, Raycast and Arc launch films, Figma Config teasers, Loom and Vercel product films share a structure: a one-line hook, a one-line problem, the product reveal, then three to six short product beats with real UI, tight camera pushes, a cursor doing one thing per beat, large editorial captions, and a closing tagline. Music carries rhythm; no voiceover is needed. Beats run 4 to 8 seconds. Nothing on screen is decoration; every shot answers "what does it do".

## Creative direction

- **Brand, not mascot-cartoon.** The dashboard's own look: cream `#f7f7f2`, ink `#17201d`, coral `#ee7375`, the near-black sidebar, the lowercase `ghostloop` wordmark with coral "oo", and the hand-drawn line-art ghost from `apps/dashboard/public/assets/ghostloop-mascot.png`. Simulated users are small line-art ghosts drawn in the same style (SVG, original), not the promo's cartoon ghosts.
- **Real UI.** Screenshots of the live dashboard and the Matrix Coor onboarding prototype at 1440×900 @2x, captured by `promo/launch/capture.mjs`, placed in a window frame and driven with GSAP camera moves, coral annotations, and a cursor. Annotation positions come from recorded element boxes (`assets/ui/boxes.json`), so highlights land on real controls.
- **One caption at a time.** Large Inter Extra Bold editorial captions, one idea each.
- **Honesty.** The results beat uses the dashboard's sample comparison (the live stack has no read-only route to a finished comparison) and carries a small "Illustrative sample result" label, as the earlier promos did. No conversion or performance claims.

## Sequence (about 70 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

| Time | Beat | On screen | Caption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Hook | Cream. Type stacks in. | You're about to launch. |
| 3–7 | Problem | Line-art ghost peeks in from the edge. | Nobody has used it yet. |
| 7–11 | Reveal | Wordmark draws in, ghost settles beside it. | ghostloop. Your first users, before your first real user. |
| 11–18 | Describe | Dashboard "What should someone be able to do?" setup; task types itself into the field; cursor presses Start. | Describe what a user should be able to do. |
| 18–29 | Simulate | Matrix Coor window. Three small ghosts arrive; the cursor fills signup, screens advance to Choose your plan; two ghosts drift to the orange "Start free trial", one hesitates on Free. | AI users try your real app. In a real browser. |
| 29–36 | Watch | Dashboard runs view: trial list, screenshot replay, action timeline; camera pans down the timeline; "50% · 2 of 4 trials reached the goal". | Every step recorded. Success checked from app state, not the agent's word. |
| 36–44 | Propose | Recommendations: "Where people may be getting stuck", suggestion card with the label change; push in on the suggestion. | Finds the pattern. Proposes one small change. |
| 44–50 | Approve | Review: before → after, "1 change selected", cursor presses "Test the selected improvements". | Nothing changes until you approve it. |
| 50–58 | Measure | Results: "How behavior changed", Accepted, 50% → 66.7%, +16.7 pp counts up; behavior table. | Retested under the same task. Measured, not guessed. |
| 58–63 | Figma | The current/updated side-by-side cards slide into a Figma-style frame with "Design match · Verified". | Every tested version lands in Figma, editable. |
| 63–70 | Loop + end | Six words around a drawn loop (Simulate · Check · Propose · Approve · Retest · Figma), ghost runs the loop, collapses into the wordmark and tagline. | Your first users, before your first real user. |

## Technique

GSAP master timeline (paused, seekable), one HTML stage at 1920×1080, Playwright seeks exact 1/30 s timestamps and pipes JPEG frames into FFmpeg (H.264, BT.709, yuv420p). Audio is an original synthesized bed generated by `promo/launch/audio.py` (numpy): warm pulse at ~104 BPM, plucked motif, swells at the reveal and the results, soft hit on each caption. Mixed and loudness-normalized with FFmpeg into the final MP4.
