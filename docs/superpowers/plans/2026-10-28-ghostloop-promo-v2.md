# GhostLoop motion-first revision

User-authorized revision: declutter, increase motion, keep the ghosts, drop code into a box that builds an app, then pop up personalities and swirl a swarm into the app. Preserve the original six-step story. Execute inline through a completed MP4.

Design: one continuous object journey, 32 seconds, 1080p/30fps. One brief caption at a time. Remove header, footer, step numbers, bullet lists, paragraph cards, and redundant UI detail. Warm cream/coral base, deep green during the swarm, signature original ghosts.

Motion: anticipation, squash/stretch at impacts, elastic assembly, curved travel, depth through scale and overlap, a contracting ghost vortex, match transitions between the box/app and app/findings, overshoot then readable holds. GSAP controls every frame; FFmpeg renders the result.

Timing:
- 0–4s: app.tsx drops through a box opening; compression, build particles, and a pulse.
- 4–7s: box transforms into a frontend with animated navigation. “Ready to test.”
- 7–10s: three large personalities pop in; a smaller swarm follows.
- 10–14s: ghosts orbit and spiral into the screen, diminishing in scale as they enter.
- 14–19s: camera moves into the app; ghosts encounter coral friction points and bounce/hesitate. “Find the friction.”
- 19–23s: findings become two compact suggestions, “Show Free” and “Clear next step.” A visible approval pulse applies the changes.
- 23–28s: ghosts flow smoothly through the updated UI; three coral markers resolve to one. Small “Illustrative demo” caption accompanies this sample result.
- 28–32s: ghosts flow back out into a brand mark. “Better before launch.” Hold.

References: Ordinary Folk Introducing Spline and Webflow A New Era of No-Code; BUCK Nothing Playground. Reviewed public project descriptions and sampled Spline video frames. No conversion or sales efficacy claims. No reference footage or artwork is used in the output.
