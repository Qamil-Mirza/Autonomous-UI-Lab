# GhostLoop

**Your first users, before your first real user.**

![GhostLoop in 30 seconds: drop in your app, AI testers try it, GhostLoop shows where they get stuck, you approve a fix, and they test it again.](docs/media/ghostloop-promo.gif)

Before you launch, nobody has used your app yet. So you can't see where people will get confused, click the wrong thing, or give up. GhostLoop lets you find out first.

## How it works

1. **Drop in your app.**
2. **AI testers try it.** They use it in a real browser, the way a person would.
3. **See where they get stuck.** GhostLoop shows exactly where they hesitate, go back, or pick the wrong button.
4. **Approve a fix.** It suggests small changes, like clearer button text. Nothing changes unless you say yes.
5. **Test it again.** The testers try the new version, so you know whether the fix really helped.

## Why it matters

Most teams discover confusing spots after launch, when real customers get stuck and leave. GhostLoop helps you catch and fix them before anyone sees them.
